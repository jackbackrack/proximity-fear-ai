**Charge-enable hardware check** (ov-power reversed-cell WARNING):

- **Topology.** Captured nets: `CHG_PROG` = {`u1.PROG`, `r_prog.p1`}, `CHG_PROG_EN` = {`r_prog.p2`, `q_en.D`}, `q_en.S` = GND. The BSS138 really opens PROG when it is off. MCP73831 p.13 §4.3: "If the PROG pin is open or floating, the MCP73831/2 are disabled". p.4 gives Minimum Shutdown Impedance 70–200 kΩ, and the off FET leaks ≤ 0.5 µA at 50 V (BSS138 p.2 IDSS), i.e. megohms at ~1 V.
- **Default on.** `CHG_EN` = {`q_en.G`, `r_en_pu.p1` (100 k to 3V3), nRF P1.13}. 3V3 exists whenever VBUS does (VBUS → SS14 → SYS → AP2112K), and the nRF's GPIOs reset to inputs, so charging is on with no firmware.
- **Gate drive.** VGS = 3.25–3.35 V against VGS(th) ≤ 1.5 V (BSS138 p.2).
- **R_PROG error.** RDS(on) is ≤ 3.5 Ω max at VGS 10 V (p.2) and about 2.9 Ω typical at 2.75 V (Fig. 6). Assuming ≤ 5 Ω, 4.99 kΩ + 5 Ω shifts IREG by ≤ 0.1 % (`power.py:72-78`). That is inside the ±1 % resistor and ±10 % IREG (p.3).
- **Detectability.** A reversed pack with USB pins VBAT at roughly the q_rev |VGS(th)| of 0.5–1.3 V (AO3401A p.2), so VBAT_SENSE reads ≤ ~0.75 V, well under the "< ~2 V" firmware threshold. VBAT cannot go negative: the q_rev body diode blocks it and the channel needs VBAT > |VGS(th)|. The AIN is therefore safe.
