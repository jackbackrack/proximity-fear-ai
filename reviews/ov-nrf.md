# ov-nrf: outside-voice review of the nRF52840 / E73 support (read-only)

Overall: no CRITICAL findings. Pad-to-port mapping, geometry, power-domain handling, reset, SWD and GPIO-pool rules check out against the PDFs. One WARNING (VDD decoupling), five NOTEs.

## Findings

WARNING — proximity_fear/circuits/nrf_support.py:126-129 (c_vcc 4.7 uF at module VCC); docstring :35-38 — External 4.7 uF at pad 19 stacks on the module's own 4.7 uF on VDD (C12) and 4.7 uF on VDDH (C11), which is the same net here (VDD = VDDH = 3V3), plus 3 x 100 nF. Nominal total is about 14 uF. The nRF52840 PS limits "effective decoupling capacitance on the VDD pin" to 2.7 / 4.7 / 5.5 uF (min/typ/max), and Nordic's config 3 uses a single 4.7 uF (C6). After X5R DC-bias derating the total probably still exceeds 5.5 uF. REG0 is off in normal-voltage mode, so the practical risk is low, but the "module already has the caps" argument in the docstring argues against adding more. The VDD-pin limit is reached by the module alone. Suggest dropping c_vcc or reducing it to about 1 uF, or recording the exception. — Evidence: PS Table 5.3.8.1, p.80 (CVDD); Ebyte manual p.11 (PDF p.12) C11, C12, C23, C25, C4; PS Fig. 212 p.589 (one 4.7 uF, C6, on VDD_nRF).

NOTE — proximity_fear/circuits/nrf_support.py:44-47, 81-82 — The comments call R1 4R7 "the config-3 BOM ... (PS p.586)". Table 149 on p.586 is circuit configuration no. 1. The config-3 BOM (Table 153, p.590) and Figure 212 (p.589) contain no R1. The recommendation for the resistor is the p.583 bullet; its value comes from the config-1 BOM. The design choice is valid, only the citation is wrong. — Evidence: PS pp.583, 586, 589, 590.

NOTE — proximity_fear/circuits/nrf_support.py:83-91, 148-160; designs/badge.py:359-362 — LFXO is within limits. Crystal Q13FC13500004 (FC-135) has CL 12.5 pF, R1 70 kOhm max, C0 1 pF, drive 0.5 uW max. PS LFXO limits are CL <= 12.5 pF, RS <= 100 kOhm, C0 <= 2 pF, drive 0.5 uW, Cpin 4 pF. With 18 pF and Cpin 4 pF, CL = (18 + 4 + Cpcb) / 2 = 12.0 pF at the assumed Cpcb of 2 pF. The margin is only 0.5 pF (Cpcb up to 3 pF). Cpcb is borrowed from Nordic's own layout, but here the caps sit about 6 mm of trace beyond XL1/XL2 pads (module pads at board y of about 2.6, caps at y = -4.0), plus module-internal stray. The PS recommends a lower-CL crystal (a 9 pF part with 12 pF caps as in Nordic's reference). Exceeding by 0.5 pF only causes a few ppm shift against a 500 ppm BLE tolerance, so this is not blocking. — Evidence: Q13FC13500004.pdf p.1; PS pp.86-87, 97 (LFXO table); PS Table 153 p.590 (X2 CL 9 pF, C17/C18 12 pF).

NOTE — proximity_fear/designs/badge.py:359-363 — The placement is untested here: XL1 (pad 11) and XL2 (pad 13) go to c_xl[0] at x -1.6 and c_xl[1] at x +1.0. After the module's 180-degree rotation on Bottom, whether the pad-to-cap routes cross depends on the bottom-side mirror convention, which I could not verify from source. Check the realized geometry. Also c_vcc (-4.6, 0.3) is about 5 mm of trace from pad 19, not "by pad 19" as the comment says. — Evidence: inference.

NOTE — proximity_fear/circuits/nrf_pins.py:90-95 (fast pool) — P0.26 (pad 12, in the inner bottom-pad row) sits between the XL1 and XL2 pads (11 and 13). It is in the fast pool, so the solver may put LED data (about 800 kHz) or PDM CLK there, next to the 32.768 kHz crystal pads. No PS rule forbids it. Consider removing P0.26 from the fast pool, or keeping it for slow signals. — Evidence: Ebyte p.7 drawing and p.8 pin table (pad 12 = P0.26); inference for the coupling concern.

NOTE — proximity_fear/circuits/nrf_pins.py:30 — The docstring says "GPIO x6" but designs/badge.py:292 requires 7 (led_en, ir_rx, ir_rx_en, tof_xshut, tof_int, button, chg_stat). It is a stale comment only, and the pool still fits (14 fast pins plus the LF pins). — Evidence: badge.py:292.

## Checked and clean (with page refs)

1. Pad-to-port mapping, all 43 pads. ebyte_E73_2G4M08S1CX.py:262-306 versus the Ebyte pin table (pp.7-9) and the "corresponding chip pin" column, cross-checked against the PS aQFN73 table (Table 142, pp.575-578). Every pad matches. Examples: 4 = AI4 = P0.28 (B11); 7 = AI0 = P0.02; 15 = AI3 = P0.05; 18 = AI2 = P0.04; 20 = "P12" = P0.12; 28 = "P15" = P0.15; 30 = "P17" = P0.17; 36 = P1.00 (AD22); 37 = SWDIO; 39 = SWDCLK; 41/43 = NF1/NF2 = P0.09/P0.10 (L24/J24); 26 = P0.18/RESET. The Ebyte p.8 text "37 SWD: clock" is a typo (chip pin SWDIO, AC24 on the p.11 schematic). The model is right.
2. Pad counts. 43 pads: 28 castellated (10 right, 8 top, 10 left) plus 15 bottom pads (7 in the row 12-24, 8 in the column 28-42), matching the drawing on p.7.
3. Geometry against the p.7 drawing:
   - Pins 1-10: pin 10 is 2.60 below the top edge, 1.27 pitch, pin 1 lowest.
   - Pins 26/27/29...43: 26 is 2.60 from the top, then 9 x 1.27, leaving 3.97 at the bottom edge.
   - Top row 11-25: 2.06 from the side edge, 1.27 pitch.
   - Bottom-pad row: 2.1 from the top edge, pin 12 at 2.69 from the side edge, 1.27 pitch.
   - Bottom-pad column: 2.1 from the left edge, pin 28 at 4.505 from the top, 1.27 pitch. 42 ends 4.605 from the bottom, which agrees.
   - Even and odd interleaving is right (pad 12 sits at the midpoint of 11 and 13, 28 at the midpoint of 27 and 29).
   - The top-view orientation (11 rightmost on the top edge, 25 leftmost, 26 top-left, 1 bottom-right) matches the top-view drawing on p.7.
   - Land pads (0.9 x 2.0 castellated, 0.9 x 0.9 bottom) overlap the module's 0.65 x 0.8 and 0.8 x 0.8 contacts. Copper-to-copper gap between the castellated land pads and the bottom pads is 0.65 mm. No overlaps.
   - Body 13 x 18 matches the drawing. On the board the E73 body corners are at r of about 21.6 mm and the IPEX keep-clear corner at 23.77 mm, both inside the 24 mm usable radius (badge.py:109-111, 195-203).
4. Power domains in normal-voltage mode. PS 5.3.1 (p.79): VDD shorted to VDDH selects normal-voltage mode, and REG0 is then disabled automatically. PS Fig. 212 (p.589, checked at 400 dpi): VDDH joins VDD and DCCH is an unconnected stub. The code does the same: P3V3 = VCC + VDDH (nrf_support.py:123) and DCCH no_connect (:124). The 3V3 rail is within VDD 1.7-3.6 V (PS p.80). Ebyte p.11 internal schematic: DEC1-DEC6 caps, REG1 DC/DC inductors (10 uH plus 15 nH from DCC to DEC4), 4.7 uF on DECUSB (C22), no capacitor on VBUS. Nothing is left out for DEC pins.
5. VBUS. 4.7 ohm in series (nrf_support.py:134-136) followed by 4.7 uF at pad 27 (:137-142). This matches the PS recommendation (p.583) and C21 4.7 uF (Table 153, p.590). The 16 V minimum rating suits 5 V VBUS. VBUS operating range 4.35-5.5 V (PS p.80-ish table), abs max 5.8 V (p.612). Series drop of 2.4 mA x 4.7 ohm is negligible.
6. Reset. P0.18 on pad 26 goes straight to TC2030 nRESET, with no pull-up or capacitor, as in Fig. 212. PSELRESET (PS p.45) must be set to 18 and both registers must be equal. The firmware requirement is documented in the code. A capacitor would stretch tPINR (p.81), and none is fitted.
7. Programming/debug. TC2030 mapping (tagconnect_TC2030.py:131-140): 1 VCC, 2 SWDIO, 3 nRESET, 4 SWCLK, 5 GND, 6 SWO. This matches the TC2030-CTX 6-pin table. Pad geometry (3 columns x 1.27, rows 1.27, holes at -2.54 and +2.54 / +-1.016, 5.08 apart) matches TC2030-IDC-NL-FP. nrf_support.py:166-169: SWDIO to pad 37, SWDCLK to pad 39, SWO to P1.00 (pad 36), nRESET to pad 26. No external pulls (DAP has internal SWDIO pull-up and SWDCLK pull-down of 13 kOhm; PS p.51-53). badge.py:338 applies the 0.508 mm track clearance tag.
8. GPIO pool rules. The 14 "standard drive, low frequency only" pins in low_frequency_gpios() match Table 142 exactly (P0.02, 03, 28, 29, 30, 31; P1.02, 04, 06, 10, 11, 13; P0.09, 10). The AIN map (AIN0-7 = P0.02, 03, 04, 05, 28, 29, 30, 31) matches. Fast signals (I2C, PDM, LED data, IR TX) are required only from FastGPIO, which excludes the LF pins. VBAT sense uses ADC (AIN-capable). The LF-only pins also include the NFC pins. Excluded from the pools: P0.00/01 (XL), P0.09/10 (NFC), P0.18 (RESET), P1.00 (SWO).
9. LFXO pin choice. XL1 = P0.00 = pad 11 (D2), XL2 = P0.01 = pad 13 (F2). The Ebyte schematic shows no 32.768 kHz crystal or caps in the module, which agrees with the manual statement on p.6 that the crystal is external.
10. Absolute-maximum and electrical limits for the reviewed nets. GPIO VI/O <= VDD + 0.3 V, VDD abs max 3.9 V, NFC pin current 80 mA (PS p.612). No overvoltage is created by the reviewed connections.
