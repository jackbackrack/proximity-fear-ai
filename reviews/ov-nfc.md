# Outside-voice RF review: NFC front end

Verdict: no CRITICAL findings. 3 WARNING, 5 NOTE.

WARNING — proximity_fear/circuits/nfc.py:43-48,56 — L_ant = 3 uH is the only input that sets the result. It is unverified and the Molex drawing gives no inductance. Sensitivity from the PS formula: +/-10 % in L moves f_res about +/-5 % (+/-0.68 MHz) and moves the ideal C_tune by about 9 pF per pin. A 1 uH error would be worse. 82 pF is only a starting point. Fit variants for the first articles (for example 56/68/82/100 pF in 0402/0603 C0G) or plan to measure. Measure with the antenna on the actual stack: case back, ferrite, LiPo, board. — evidence: PS p.215 equations; inference for the sensitivity.

WARNING — proximity_fear/circuits/nfc.py:84-92 — The capacitor query sets no tolerance. C0G parts of +/-5 % are 82 pF +/-4.1 pF each, which unbalances the legs. The PS requires the total capacitance on NFC1 and NFC2 to be matched (PS p.215). The effect is modest: a mismatch of dC gives about dC/2 on C', or about 0.5 %/pF in f. A 4 pF mismatch gives about 2 % in f and some common-mode imbalance. Add `tolerance=AtMost(0.02)`, or accept +/-5 % and note it. — evidence: PS p.215; inference.

WARNING — proximity_fear/designs/badge.py:353-356 — NFC1 and NFC2 are not constrained to equal length or parasitic capacitance. The two caps sit at (-9.3,14.3) and (-9.3,17.0), with the pads at (-14.25,15.5) and (-11.75,15.5). The caps are placed asymmetrically, so trace length and the capacitance to the L3 and Bottom GND copper will differ per leg. Check the routed NFC1 and NFC2 copper after build (length and area), and keep both legs short, equal and over the same plane. I did not see the routed geometry, so this is not confirmed. — evidence: PS p.215 "must be matched"; inference.

NOTE — proximity_fear/circuits/nfc.py:1-12,43-48 — Topology and formula match the PS. Figure 66 shows C_tune and C_p as shunts to GND on each pin, in series around the loop. The equations are C' = 1/((2 pi 13.56 MHz)^2 L_ant), C' = (C_p + C_int + C_tune)/2, and C_tune = 2/((2 pi 13.56 MHz)^2 L_ant) - C_p - C_int. Recomputed: 2/(7.259e15 x 3e-6) = 91.8 pF. Less 8 pF gives 83.8 pF, so 82 pF. C' = 45 pF and f = 13.70 MHz (+1 %). The 2 uH check gives 137.8 pF less 8 pF = about 130 pF, as in the PS. The PS gives no numeric C_int. The 4 pF in the code comes from the DevZone blog, which is outside the packet. The only PS figure is C_PAD_NFC = 4 pF (p.148 electrical spec), and that is stated for GPIO mode. Treat C_int as an estimate. — evidence: PS p.215 Fig. 66; PS electrical spec table (CPAD_NFC).

NOTE — proximity_fear/components/antennas/nfc_pads.py:51-54 and proximity_fear/substrate.py — C_p over the plane is plausible. Each land is 2.0 x 3.5 mm = 7 mm^2 over the 0.0764 mm 1080 prepreg to L3 GND. The jitxlib JLC04161H_1080 file confirms 0.0764 mm. With er about 3.9, C = e0 er A/d = 3.2 pF before fringing, so 4 pF per pin is reasonable. Both pads are identical, so the contribution is symmetric. Do not clear GND under them. Clearing L3 would cut about 3 pF per pad and force a retune, and it gains little at 13.56 MHz. If it were done, clear both pads identically. The Bottom GND pour is auto-clearanced around the pads, which adds only a small symmetric amount. — evidence: inference. Prepreg thickness is from the jitxlib source; the stackup values were not checked against the fab PDF.

NOTE — antenna on ferrite near the LiPo — The ferrite sheet is the intended shield against the LiPo and the 50 mm board's L3 and Bottom GND planes. The listed L is probably the free or ferrite-backed value. In the final stack, conductive material (foil pouch, planes) can lower L and Q. Retune on the final assembly. Plan to measure the resonance with the LiPo present. — evidence: inference; the Molex drawing p.1 has no L, Q or tuning data.

NOTE — ESD on the antenna pads — The PS gives no external-ESD requirement for the NFC pins. Abs max (p.612) lists only I_NFC1/2 = 80 mA, ESD HBM 2 kV and CDM 750 V for the aQFN package. With NFCPINS.PROTECT=NFC, the internal clamp shorts NFC1 and NFC2 when the differential voltage exceeds about 2 V (PS 6.14.3, p.214). That covers strong-field excess voltage, not external ESD. The antenna is hand-soldered and sits inside the case, so exposure is only at assembly. No external TVS is needed. If one is added, it must be a matched pair under 0.5 pF that is included in C_p. — evidence: PS p.214, p.612.

NOTE — proximity_fear/circuits/nfc.py:30-40 and badge.py:273-274 — Pin rules are met. NFC1 and NFC2 are tied only to the pads and the tuning caps (nrf.nfc1/nrf.nfc2 fixed to P0.09/P0.10). They are not used as GPIO anywhere in badge.py. The PS says the UICR NFCPINS reset value is 0xFFFFFFFF and PROTECT = 1 selects NFC, so the erased default is NFC protect (PS 4.5.1.6, p.45). The PS says PROTECT=Disabled removes the strong-field protection and the NFCT never leaves DISABLE (PS 6.14.3, p.214). Firmware must not write PROTECT=Disabled. Check that the bootloader or SDK config does not set it (for example `CONFIG_NFCT_PINS_AS_GPIOS`). Section 6.14.11 (p.215) says to put a series diode in the battery path if the battery cannot take return current in a strong field. The PS places no condition on the battery input here, and the LiPo is on the PowerSupply side, so confirm the reverse FET covers this. This was not in scope. — evidence: PS pp.45, 214, 215.

NOTE — Pad geometry versus the Molex drawing — The Molex 1462360001 PSD sheet 1 shows two solder pads of 2.00 x 3.00 mm ("2.00 x2", "3.00 X2") with a 0.50 gap, which gives a 2.50 mm pitch. The code uses 2.0 x 3.5 mm lands at 2.5 mm pitch, which is consistent. Row 1462360111 gives A = 23 and B = 27.3 with ferrite, and thickness 0.27 +/- 0.07 mm. The drawing has no polarity, so p1 and p2 are interchangeable. At (-13,15.5), the courtyard corner is about r = 23.4 mm, inside the 24 mm rim limit (badge.py:86). Not blocking. — evidence: Molex drawing p.1.
