# Outside-voice power-tree review (read-only; checked against the PDFs, not the .spec.md notes)

Result: no CRITICAL findings. 3 WARNING, 6 NOTE. Page refs are the datasheet's own printed page numbers.

## Findings

WARNING — proximity_fear/circuits/power.py:95-146 (UsbC), :148-199 (Charger), :244-267 (Ldo3V3); nrf_support.py:132-143 — Unprotected VBUS hot-plug overshoot reaches parts with low abs-max. The only VBUS clamp is the USBLC6, which does not clamp below 6 V (VBR min 6 V at 1 mA, no VBUS clamp curve; USBLC6 p.2 Table 2). Only the nRF has a series resistor. About 9.5 µF of ceramic sits on VBUS, plus 1 µF on SYS behind the SS14. The SS14 acts as a peak detector: SYS holds whatever peak VBUS reaches. With a cable inductance of about 1 µH and ESR of about 0.3 Ω, ζ is about 0.5 and the first overshoot is about 1.2× to 1.9×, which is 6 to 9 V. Against that: AP2112K VIN recommended max 6.0 V and abs max 6.5 V (p.3); MCP73831 VDD operating max 6 V and abs max 7.0 V (p.3); and with LEDs ON, WS2812C VDD abs max 5.3 V (p.2), because VLED follows SYS through the on FET. A plug-in with the cell present and LEDs lit is the worst case, since the LED switch is then conducting. Suggested mitigation: TVS (about 5.5-6 V standoff) on VBUS and/or small series R or ferrite ahead of the charger/SS14. Evidence: datasheet limits above; the overshoot figure is inference.

WARNING — proximity_fear/circuits/power.py:269-310 (Battery, Q_rev, gate to GND) — The reverse-battery FET fails to protect when VBUS is present. With USB in and no cell, the MCP73831 drives VBAT to about 4.2 V or more. Plugging a reversed cell then gives Q_rev VGS = 0 - VBAT = -4.2 V, so it turns ON (VGS(th) -0.5 to -1.3 V, AO3401A p.2) and connects the reversed cell, at about -4.2 V, to VBAT. The charger collapses into preconditioning, about 10% of IREG (~20 mA), and reverse-charges the cell indefinitely. The FET dissipates about 0.1 W. The protection is only valid when nothing else holds VBAT up. JST-PH cells with swapped leads are common, so this is a real field case. Suggested mitigation: put the FET source on the cell side, or add a series resistor or fuse; otherwise document that it only protects the no-USB case. Evidence: AO3401A p.1-2; MCP73831 p.3-4 (preconditioning, no-battery behaviour); inference for the sequence.

WARNING — ARCHITECTURE.md:61 (standby claim), power.py:20-24, centre_sensors.py:16-21 — The "≲ 0.1 mA System OFF" budget does not close at the datasheet maxima, and omits items. Tally from the PDFs:
- AP2112K IQ is 55 µA typ and 80 µA MAX (p.8, 3.3 V table). The notes quote only 55.
- VL53L4CX SW standby is 6 µA typ and 9 µA max (DS13805 p.18 Table 12).
- VBAT divider is 3.7 V / 2 MΩ = 1.9 µA, up to 2.1 µA at 4.2 V.
- nRF System OFF is 0.4 µA, or 1.9 µA with RAM retention (PS p.56 sleep table).
- MCP73831 reverse leakage is 0.25 µA (p.4).
- LMD3526 mic: VDD is always on, and the datasheet gives NO sleep-mode current (only CLK < 50 kHz = "sleep", p.4). Active is 320 to 750 µA. The mic is absent from the standby budget.
- SS14 reverse leakage at 3.7 V: only 0.3 mA at 40 V / 25 °C and 10 mA at 100 °C are specified (SS14 p.1). Nothing is given at 3.7 V. It feeds the 10 k bleed on VBUS.
Total: about 65-75 µA typical plus unknown mic and SS14 terms. At maxima it is about 94 µA before the mic and SS14, so there is essentially no margin. Typical is plausible; guaranteed ≲ 0.1 mA is unproven. Firmware must also stop PDM CLK (low) and park every GPIO before SYSTEMOFF.

NOTE — power.py:148-199, ARCHITECTURE.md:13-15 — USB budget arithmetic is thin and ignores unit loads. IREG is 200 mA nominal with PROG tolerance of ±10% (MCP73831 p.3), so up to 220 mA. Add 130 mA on 3V3 (peak), 150 mA LEDs, nRF VBUS 2.4 mA (PS p.568), 3 mA charge LED, 0.5 mA gate bleed, and the total is about 506 mA at the corners. That is a few mA over 500 mA, though it is unlikely that the 130 mA peak and the 150 mA LED cap coincide with full charge current. Before enumeration, a host allows only 100 mA; the charger starts at 200 mA with no firmware involvement. Failure is benign: a sagging VBUS turns Q_bat on (VGS = VBUS - SYS goes negative) and the cell assists. Also, the LED cap is firmware-only; there is no hardware limit (750 mA all-white). Evidence: MCP73831 p.3; PS p.568; inference.

NOTE — power.py:213-227, led_rings.py:41-44, ARCHITECTURE.md:15,17 — SYS and VLED range on USB is slightly stated low. The table says 3.0 to 4.9 V. With VBUS 5.25 V and SS14 VF at light LED load of about 0.2 to 0.3 V (VF is only given as 0.55 V at 1 A, SS14 p.1), SYS reaches about 5.0 V. That is still under the WS2812C 5.3 V abs max (p.2) but only by about 0.3 V. At the same time WS2812C VIH is 0.55 × VDD = 2.75 V (p.3, tabled at 5 V) against nRF VOH,SD min = VDD - 0.4 = 2.85 V at the AP2112K's minimum of 3.2505 V (AP2112K p.8; PS p.156, VOH,SD). The margin is only about 0.1 V at the extreme. Arithmetic works but is thin: consider H0H1 (high drive) or a 74-series buffer on DIN. Evidence: WS2812C p.2-3; AP2112K p.8; PS GPIO table.

NOTE — power.py:213-227, led_rings.py:71-83 — Unplug and plug transients upset the LEDs. After USB removal SYS = VBAT - VSD, about 0.65 V at 430 mA (AO3401A p.2, VSD -0.7 V typ at 1 A), for the estimated 25-65 ms until Q_bat turns on. At VBAT 3.7 V that is about 3.0 V, and at 3.4 V it is about 2.75 V. This is below the WS2812C operating minimum of 3.3 V (p.2), so the chain may glitch or reset while lit. 3V3 stays above the nRF 1.7 V and ToF 2.6 V minimums. It is cosmetic; no damage.

NOTE — power.py:269-285 (battery detect) — The VBAT sense divider is 2 × 1 MΩ at 1% = 1.98 MΩ worst case. MCP73831 ZNO_BAT requires VBAT impedance ≥ 2 MΩ for reliable no-battery detection (p.4). The comment treats 2 MΩ as passing; at tolerance it is 20 kΩ short. The effect is only on STAT behaviour without a cell. Harmless, but unmargined; use 1.1 MΩ pairs if no-battery detect matters.

NOTE — power.py:148-199 — Charger dissipation is thermally limited. At VDD = 5.25 V and VBAT = 3.0 V, 0.22 A, P is about 0.5 W (0.34 W at 3.7 V). The SOT-23-5 θJA is 230 °C/W minimum copper and about 130 °C/W with large copper (MCP73831 p.5), so the rise is about 45-110 °C. Thermal regulation will cut charge current in a closed case, so charge time is longer than 0.4C. This is not a failure. AP2112K: (5.05 - 3.3) × 0.13 A = 0.23 W × 184 °C/W (SOT25, p.3) = 42 °C rise; fine. Dropout is 200 mV max at 300 mA (p.8), so 3V3 regulates down to about VBAT 3.4-3.55 V as claimed.

NOTE — power.py:269-285, led_rings.py:41-43 — There is no hardware undervoltage lockout. LEDs and VLED follow the cell to 3.0 V and below (WS2812C minimum 3.3 V, p.2) if firmware stalls, and cell over-discharge protection depends on the cell's own PCM. nRF VDD recommended min 1.7 V (PS p.611), so the MCU keeps running. Add a note that the cell must carry a PCM.

## Checked and consistent (no finding)

- AP2112K: VIN 2.5-6.0 V, VOUT 3.3 V ±1.5% (3.2505-3.3495 V), 600 mA, dropout 125/200 mV at 300 mA, EN VIH 1.5 V min with EN = VIN (p.3, p.8). 3V3 max stays under nRF VDD abs max 3.9 V and recommended 3.6 V (PS p.611-612), ToF AVDD 3.5 V recommended and 3.6 V abs max (DS13805 p.17 Tables 9-10), and mic VDD 3.6 V.
- Q_bat / Q_rev: VGS ±12 V, VDS -30 V (AO3401A p.1); RDS(on) 85 mΩ at -2.5 V gives 37 mV at 430 mA. LED switch on-state VGS -3.1 to -4.45 V is inside rating. Q_bat gate on VBUS: VGS reaches at most +VBUS with SYS at 0 V, inside ±12 V.
- Q_bat body diode in USB mode: SYS ≥ 4.75 - 0.55 = 4.2 V against VBAT ≤ 4.232 V (MCP73831 p.3); no conduction. SS14 leakage into the VBUS bleed stays under the nRF VBUS,DETECT minimum of 3.4 V (PS p.82) at the quoted 0.3 mA × 10 k = 3 V worst case.
- nRF VBUS: 4.35-5.5 V operating, 5.8 V abs (PS p.611-612). The 4.7 Ω series resistor is per the PS reference circuit. (The PS lists 2 Ω max VBUS source resistance, p.82, which the 4.7 Ω slightly exceeds; the PS's own reference design includes it, so this is acceptable.)
- Back-power through data pins: WS2812C DIN abs max is VDD + 0.7 V (p.2), so DIN must be driven low whenever VLED is off. The firmware rule exists; VLED discharges slowly (about 1 s, 50 µA static), so the exposure is limited. ToF I/O is failsafe with no diode to AVDD (DS13805 p.17), and every I/O is at 3V3 anyway. IRM OUT: the supply-off case is covered by the documented firmware rule. USB D+/D- are not driven when VBUS is absent (PS p.568-569).
- IR TX: 46 mA worst case is under IF 65 mA (IR11-21C p.3); the 47 Ω at 0.1 W fits 0805 0.125 W. IRM: Vs 2.7-5.5 V, Icc 0.4/0.7 mA, VH = Vcc - 0.4 V (IRM p.3).
- ToF active 21 mA avg, 40 mA peak (DS13805 p.18 Table 12); IR TX and ToF are firmware-exclusive, so the 130 mA 3V3 peak is plausible (radio 16.4 mA is the DC/DC figure, PS p.58; LDO mode is higher, about 28 mA at +8 dBm by scaling from the 0 dBm figures, 10.8 vs 6.4 mA; still fits with ToF and IR exclusive, though with no margin).
- Pinouts and mappings of AP2112K, MCP73831 and AO3401A match their datasheets. SS14 orientation relies on an LCSC override (mdd_SS14.py:97-103) and the model's pad numbers are opposite to the real part; the design is rev-2 fab-proven, but check the CPL polarity on the first PCBA.
- Centre-disc and annulus pour geometry in badge.py is consistent: the 3V3 disc is r 7.75 and the VLED hole is r 8.25, the 3V3 vias (r about 6.3 and 7.1) fall inside the disc, and the VLED vias at r 12.9 sit on the annulus. No blocking geometry mismatch in the reviewed files.
