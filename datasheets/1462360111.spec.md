# 1462360111 - Molex, 23 x 27.3 mm ferrite-backed flex NFC antenna (13.56 MHz)

Source: `datasheets/1462360111.pdf` (Molex sales drawing "SD FOR RECTANGLE NFC ANTENNA", doc 1462360001 PSD rev B, released 2019/10/28; fetched from the LCSC mirror of C17350773 because molex.com reset every connection); pages read: 1 (single sheet)

## Layout, thermal and decoupling requirements
Drawing gives only the tail pad geometry (2.00 x 3.00 mm, 0.50 gap) and general tolerance +/-0.2 for 2-place dims (title block). Board lands: 2.0 x 3.5 mm at 2.5 mm pitch (tail laps on directly, 0.5 mm extra length for iron/fillet; 28 AWG lead also fits), no paste (hand-soldered after PCBA).

## Open questions
- Inductance and Q are not on the sales drawing; ~3 uH is the PLAN.md working figure. Tuning caps are trimmed by measurement on the first boards.
- Which face of the tail carries the exposed pads is not stated; lap or wire attach both work on the chosen lands.
