# BSS138-7-F - Diodes Inc., 50 V 200 mA N-channel MOSFET (gate drive, IR drive)

Source: `datasheets/BSS138.pdf` (DS30144 Rev 25-2, June 2024); pages read: 1, 2, 3, 5
Package: SOT23, D 2.80-3.00 x E1 1.20-1.40 mm, thermal pad: no (p.5)
Absolute maxima that bind this design: VDSS 50 V, VGSS +-20 V, ID 200 mA continuous, IDM 1 A (10 us, 1 %), PD 300 mW (p.2).

Electrical (p.2): VGS(th) 0.5 / 1.2 / 1.5 V min/typ/max; RDS(on) 1.4 typ / 3.5 Ohm max at VGS 10 V (expect several Ohm at 3.3 V gate drive - check IR LED current budget); Ciss 50 pF max.

## Layout, thermal and decoupling requirements
Suggested pad layout (p.5): X 0.8, Y 0.9, C 2.0, X1 1.35, Y1 2.9. Model: 1.07 x 0.6 lands on 2.48 mm rows (EasyEDA, fab-proven).

## Open questions
Resolved (cir-04): p.3 Fig.6 gives RDS(on) of about 2.9 ohm at VGS 2.75 V, 25 C, 50-150 mA (1.9 at -55 C, 4.8 at 150 C). Fig.1 shows ID > 0.35 A at VGS 3.0 V. At 3.3 V gate drive, use about 3 ohm for the IR LED budget.
