# E73-2G4M08S1CX - Ebyte, nRF52840 BLE module, IPEX antenna connector

Source: `datasheets/E73-2G4M08S1CX.pdf` (Manual v1.2), pages 5-9, 11-12 (PDF = manual + 1); PS pins and LCSC record in `datasheets/sourcing/`.
Package: 13.0 × 18.0 × 3.0 mm; 43 pads, 1.27 mm: 28 castellated + 15 bottom pads (0.8 mm square, 2.1 mm in from top/left edge) (p.7). Thermal pad: no.
Absolute maxima: supply 0-5.5 V (p.5); 5 V logic needs 1-5.1 kΩ series (p.12).

## Layout, thermal and decoupling requirements
- Avoid high-frequency digital, analog and power routing on the layer under the module; if unavoidable, grounded copper under it, route near its digital part (p.11-12).
- IPEX (50 Ω, p.6) sits centred near the short edge opposite pins 11-25 (p.7). Model adds a 6 × 3 mm courtyard beyond that edge for snapping the plug.
