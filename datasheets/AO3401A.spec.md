# AO3401A - Alpha & Omega, 30 V P-channel MOSFET (LED switch, reverse battery)

Source: `datasheets/AO3401A.pdf` (Rev 3.1 Dec 2023), `datasheets/AOS_SOT23_package.pdf` (PO-00001 Rev M); pages read: AO3401A 1-2; package 1
Package: SOT-23, D 2.80-3.10 x E1 1.40-1.80 mm, thermal pad: no
Absolute maxima that bind this design: VDS -30 V, VGS +-12 V, ID -4 A (25 C) / -3.2 A (70 C), PD 1.4 W (p.1).

## Layout, thermal and decoupling requirements
AOS recommended land 0.80 x 0.80 mm, rows 2.40 mm apart, 0.95 pitch (package p.1). Model: 1.0 x 0.6, rows 2.30 (EasyEDA, fab-proven). Reverse-battery use: drain to battery +, source to load, gate to GND (via resistor), so the body diode conducts first.
