# AP2112K-3.3TRG1 - Diodes Inc., 600 mA 3.3 V CMOS LDO with enable

Source: `datasheets/AP2112K-3.3TRG1.pdf` (DS39724 Rev 2-2); pages read: 1, 2, 3, 8, 12, 14
Package: SOT25 (SOT-23-5), body 1.50-1.70 x 2.90-3.10 mm, thermal pad: no (p.14)
Absolute maxima that bind this design: VCC 6.5 V; recommended VIN 2.5-6.0 V; theta-JA 184 C/W (SOT25) (p.3).

## Pinout
| pin | name | type | note |
|-----|------|------|------|
| 1 | VIN | power in | (p.2) |
| 2 | GND | ground | (p.2) |
| 3 | EN | input | H = on, L = shutdown, output discharged via 60 Ohm (p.1-2) |
| 4 | NC | no connect | (p.1 pin assignment) |
| 5 | VOUT | power out | 3.3 V +-1.5 % (p.8) |

## Required external components
| part | value | why | page |
|------|-------|-----|------|
| CIN | 1 uF X5R/X7R | stability | p.2 Note 4 |
| COUT | 1 uF X5R/X7R | stability | p.2 Note 4 |
| EN tie | to VIN (always on) or GPIO | internal pull-down RPD 3.0 MOhm typ only (too weak to rely on; VIH 1.5 V min) | pp.4-8 electrical tables |

Electrical (3.3 V option, p.8): IOUT >= 600 mA; dropout 125 mV typ / 200 mV max at 300 mA, 250/400 mV at 600 mA; IQ 55 uA typ / 80 max; standby 0.01 uA (EN low).

## Layout, thermal and decoupling requirements
Caps next to VIN and VOUT pins. Dissipation = (VIN - 3.3) x I; at 4.6 V SYS and 150 mA that is 0.2 W -> ~36 C rise at 184 C/W (p.3). Suggested pads Z 3.20, G 1.60, X 0.55, Y 0.80 (p.14).

## Open questions
With VBAT near 3.4 V the output follows VIN minus dropout (below 3.3 V).
