## Layout, thermal and decoupling requirements
- Recommended footprint (FC-135.pdf p.4): pads 1.0 (along axis) x 1.8 mm, 2.5 mm centre-to-centre; "Do not design any circuit patterns in the shaded area" between the pads.
- Oscillator negative resistance ≥ 5 × ESR (FC-135.pdf p.7 handling note 10): ≥ 350 kΩ.
- Shortest line pattern on PCB recommended (FC-135.pdf p.7 note 3). MSL 1 (p.4).
