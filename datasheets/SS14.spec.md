# SS14 - MDD, 40 V 1 A Schottky rectifier (power OR diode)

Source: `datasheets/SS14.pdf` (SS12 thru SS1200, Rev 2025A5); pages read: 1, 3
Package: DO-214AC (SMA), body 3.80-4.50 x 2.30-2.80 mm, thermal pad: no (p.1)
Absolute maxima that bind this design: VRRM 40 V, I(AV) 1.0 A, IFSM 25 A (8.3 ms), TJ -55..150 C (p.1).

Electrical (p.1): VF <= 0.55 V at 1.0 A; IR 0.3 mA max at 25 C, 10 mA at 100 C (rated VR); CJ 110 pF typ; RthJA 90 C/W.

## Layout, thermal and decoupling requirements
Suggested pad layout (p.3): pad 1.52 x 1.68 mm, land centres 3.93 mm, inner gap 2.41 mm. Model matches (1.52 x 1.68 on 3.92 mm).

## Open questions
Reverse leakage (up to 0.3 mA at 25 C) back-feeds the idle OR leg; acceptable for a diode-OR.
