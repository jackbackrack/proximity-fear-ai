Source: `datasheets/IR11-21C_TR8.pdf` (DIR-0000945 Rev 4, 2015-09-26); pages read: 1, 2, 3, 4

## Layout, thermal and decoupling requirements
- Recommended soldering pad (p.2): 0.9 mm wide x 1.3 mm tall pads, 1.6 mm inner gap, 3.4 mm outer (centres +-1.25); "for reference only".
- Model uses EasyEDA C19269752 footprint: 1.485 x 1.62 pads on +-1.49 mm centres, pad 1 = anode at +x; fabbed on socialbadge rev 2.
- p.4 Fig.3 (pulsed, typ): VF is about 1.0 V at 10 mA and about 1.25-1.3 V at 40-50 mA. Fig.1 derates continuous IF from 65 mA (up to about 25 C) to about 5 mA at 85 C, roughly 1 mA/C (cir-04).
- Optical (p.3): lambda_p 940 nm, radiant intensity 1.6 mW/sr typ at 20 mA, 2theta1/2 100 deg; emits from the top face, so it sits on the front.
