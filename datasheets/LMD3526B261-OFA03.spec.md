# LMD3526B261-OFA03 - LinkMems, bottom-port PDM MEMS microphone

Source: `datasheets/LMD3526B261-OFA03.pdf` (spec NO M196 Version 1.0, via LCSC C41347997); pages read: 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8 (5-8 rendered and read visually)
Package: LGA-5, 3.50 x 2.65 x 1.00 mm (±0.10), acoustic port Ø0.32 ± 0.05 mm on the bottom, thermal pad: no (p.7)
Absolute maxima that bind this design: VDD 1.62-3.6 V operating (p.4; no separate abs-max table); logic input -0.3 to VDD+0.3 V (p.4); reflow peak 260 °C, 30 s max (p.8); operating -40 to +85 °C (p.8)

## Layout, thermal and decoupling requirements
- Timing (p.5): SEL = VDD drives DATA after the CLK rising edge and goes Hi-Z after the falling edge, so the host samples on the CLK falling edge; SEL = GND drives after the falling edge, host samples on the rising edge. TDV 24-48 ns, TDZ 0-20 ns, CLK duty 45-55 %, jitter ≤ 0.5 ns rms, DATA load ≤ 140 pF (p.4).
- Recommended solder pad layout (p.8): signal pads 0.73 x 0.52 mm on 1.67 mm column pitch and 0.82 mm row pitch; GND ring Ø1.62 outer / Ø1.03 inner. Bottom view (p.7): lower pad row centre 1.25 mm from the port centre, port centre 1.04 mm from the body edge, port centred in X (1.33 of 2.65).
- Bottom port: acoustic through-hole inside the ring (diameter: Open questions).
- Handling (p.8): no washing, ultrasonic cleaning or vacuum on the port. MSL 1.

## Open questions
- PCB acoustic hole diameter not specified by LinkMems; Ø0.50 mm is a design choice (≥ port Ø0.37 max, inside ring Ø1.03).
